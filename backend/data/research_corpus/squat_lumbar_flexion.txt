Title: Lumbar Kinematics and Shear Forces During Deep Squatting
Abstract: Lumbar flexion ("butt wink") at maximal squat depth shifts mechanical load from active posterior chain musculature to passive osteoligamentous structures. Biomechanical modeling demonstrates that full lumbar spinal flexion increases shear forces on L4-L5 and L5-S1 intervertebral discs by up to 40%. Maintaining a neutral lumbar spine preserves intra-abdominal pressure efficiency and reduces anterior vertebral body compression.
