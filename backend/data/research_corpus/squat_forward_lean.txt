Title: Segmental Length Ratios and Forward Trunk Lean in Back Squats
Abstract: Excessive forward trunk lean during barbell squats alters moment arms relative to the hip and lumbar spine joints. When torso incline exceeds optimal sagittal angles, moment arms at the hip and erector spinae expand significantly, shifting load from the quadriceps to the lumbar extensors. This is often triggered by restricted ankle dorsiflexion or weak quadriceps during the concentric transition out of the bottom position.
