Title: Scapulothoracic and Glenohumeral Alignment in Pushups
Abstract: Excessive shoulder abduction (flaring elbows at 90 degrees) during pushups reduces subacromial space width and increases impaction of the supraspinatus tendon against the acromion process. Tucking elbows to 45 degrees optimizes pectoral and anterior deltoid mechanics while protecting shoulder labral structures.
