Title: Joint Kinetics across Varying Squat Depths
Abstract: Parallel and deep squats require progressive ankle dorsiflexion and hip flexion angles. Failing to achieve proper depth (stopping above parallel) limits peak gluteus maximus and adductor magnus activation, which reach maximal moment arms near 90 to 110 degrees of knee flexion. Partial depth squats also increase peak patellar tendon stress per unit of load compared to full depth.
